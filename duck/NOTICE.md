# Open Duck Mini v2
`duck/robot/` is the Open Duck Mini v2 model (MuJoCo XML, URDF, STL meshes) by Antoine Pirrone and contributors,
https://github.com/apirrone/Open_Duck_Mini, Apache-2.0 (see LICENSE-open-duck-mini). A miniature of Disney's BDX droid.
Printable parts, BOM and build guide: see that repo (`print/`, `docs/`, BOM sheet linked in its README).
